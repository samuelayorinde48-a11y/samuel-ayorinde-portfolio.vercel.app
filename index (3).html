The problem

Many websites look fine and still lose people.

A visitor lands, scans, and leaves with questions unanswered. Not because the business is weak, but because the site never answered them.

  • What exactly do they do?
  • Is this for someone like me?
  • Where do I click first?
  • Can I trust them with this?
  • The site looks good but explains little
  • Visitors work too hard to understand it
  • The message is unclear
  • Important information is buried
  • Nothing guides people to the next step
  • The site does not match the quality of the business

My approach, in the order I work

  1. StructureWho the site is for and what it must say.
  2. LayoutThe order of pages and sections, so the message arrives in the right sequence.
  3. DesignThe visual layer that makes the path easy to follow.
  4. BuildA fast, responsive site that works as planned.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What platforms do you work with?

Mainly Wix and Wix Studio. I also work with Shopify and supporting design tools when a project needs them.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I review your current site, find what needs work, and redesign its structure and look around your business goals. It can be the whole site or a few key pages.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. I check every layout on phone and tablet sizes, including text, menus, buttons and spacing.

Do you include SEO?

I set up the essentials: page titles, meta descriptions, heading order and site settings. Search results depend on more than design, so I will not promise rankings.

What happens when I book a call?

You pick a time and we talk for 30 minutes. You tell me about your business and your site. I share what I notice and how I would approach it. No pressure.

How long does a project take and what does it cost?

It depends on the number of pages and how much content you already have. After a short call I will give you a clear price and a clear timeline.
